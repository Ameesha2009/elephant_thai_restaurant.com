Our Story & Ambience

Warm Thai Hospitality in the Heart of South Charleston

At Elephant Thai Restaurant, we believe great food is about more than just taste—it’s about bringing people together with warmth and kindness. Located at 228 7th Ave, our family-owned eatery combines classic Thai culinary traditions with a vibrant, modern Sushi Bar.

Whether you are stopping in for our superb lunch specials, taking home a full dinner spread, or enjoying our sweet Thai Iced Tea, our team (led by friendly staff like Oui) strives to make every guest feel like family.
